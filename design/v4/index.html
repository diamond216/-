Who's behind this

Hamster Hub is a technology community for students. Hamster Nest is where their best work goes on show.

Read how we pick projects

  • Someone here has checked it.

    Before a project goes up, Hamster Hub looks at the work and the team behind it. The checklist is public, so you can read exactly what we look for.

  • The money part is written down.

    Each project says how it earns, who owns what, and whether it's raising. No digging through a pitch deck.

  • You can watch it move.

    Every project posts updates. The ones that post most often come first.
