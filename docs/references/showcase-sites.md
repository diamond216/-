## Patterns that came up again and again

- **Freshness on every card** ("10 min ago", "2 days left"): IndieDB, Kickstarter, Peerlist, Indie Hackers. We already do this; keep it prominent.
- **A curated row before the full grid** (Staff picks, Best of, Featured): Devpost, Behance, Steam, itch.io jams.
- **Weekly boards with a timer**: Peerlist, Indie Hackers. Strong fit for "post more, climb higher".
- **Proof on the card** (winner ribbon, % funded, upvotes): Devpost, Kickstarter, Peerlist.
- **Things to avoid:** ad blocks inside the grid (Game Jolt, IndieDB) and cookie walls covering content.
